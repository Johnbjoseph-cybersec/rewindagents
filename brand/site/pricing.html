02Questions about paying

Before you ask.

Will the open-source tool stay free?

The code you have is Apache-2.0, and that licence cannot be withdrawn from it. Nothing that exists today is behind a paywall: the recorder, the hook, replay, rewind, branch, export and sessions are all in the open-source tier.

What would Cloud add?

Things that need a service rather than a command: the same session history on every machine you and your team use, the export page hosted at a link you can hand to a reviewer, sessions kept for longer than a laptop keeps them, and search across all of them. None of it is built yet. export and sessions are the local halves and are free.

How does the waitlist work?

It is a GitHub issue on the repository with the label waitlist. Say what you would pay for. There is no form, no email list and no tracking on this site.

Can my company get an invoice or a quote today?

No. Nothing is for sale yet. When Cloud exists, this page will carry the price and a checkout link, and the link will live in one config key until then.
